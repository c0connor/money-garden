How your garden works 🌷

The idea

You track two things: money that landed (harvests 🌻) and hours you spent (watering 💧). Plots connect them — each client job or company is a plot, and fee ÷ hours shows what you really earn per hour there.

Money only counts when it's real

Invoices and finished jobs sit as “waiting” (dashed, no flower) until you mark them paid. Canceled? Remove it — your totals never lied. The garden only grows on real money.

Where your numbers live

On this Mac, in this browser. No internet, no account, no tracking. Always open the app in the same browser or it will look empty.

The one habit: Back up

Tap Back up monthly, drag the little file into iCloud Drive. The app reminds you gently.

Auto-save to a file (extra safety)

Pick a spot in iCloud Drive once and the garden saves itself after every change.

The potting shed 🪴

Your brain-dump room. Toss seeds (to-dos) in without sorting; file them into trays later or never. Today's bench holds three max — on purpose. Done seeds bloom 🌼; stale ones get composted 🍂, guilt-free. The 🎙️ button uses your device's speech service (Apple/Google) to turn talk into text — everything else stays on your device.

Sunshine ☀️

One good thing a day — typed or spoken. No streaks, no guilt; missed days don't matter, the sun comes back. Your rays ride in the same backup as everything else.
